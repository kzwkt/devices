## Overview
Made by RIM (Research In Motion), running BlackBerry OS 7.1 (bundle 2394, v7.1.0.907, platform 9.49.0.98). I kept notes on the menus, apps and a few things I loved on it, including BBM.

## Specs

| Part | Detail |
|---|---|
| OS | BlackBerry OS 7.1, bundle 2394 |
| Carrier skin | O2 |

## What was on it
- **Messaging:** messages, BBM, instant messaging (MSN, Yahoo, Google Talk), social feeds (Facebook, Twitter, YouTube)
- **Media:** music (songs, artists, albums), pictures (camera, library, wallpaper), videos, video camera, FM radio, podcasts
- **Apps:** calendar, clock, calculator, tasks, Documents To Go, files, password keeper, voice notes and voice dialing, BlackBerry Protect, App World, aerize wifix
- **Games:** Brick Breaker, Word Mole
- **Setup:** sound and alert profiles, ringtones (Spirit), manage connections
- **Ringtone/song noted:** Sally Seltmann, "Harmony To My Heartbeat"
